# Concept Design

## Purpose

Develop a coherent interior-design concept that aligns project goals, user needs, and spatial decisions.

## Basic Rules

1. Analyze the design goals, including project type, desired experience, constraints, and priorities.
2. Analyze the users' needs, activities, preferences, accessibility requirements, and operational expectations.
3. Extract a concise, distinctive design concept that can guide subsequent decisions.
4. Establish spatial design logic by connecting functional zoning, circulation, hierarchy, atmosphere, and material or lighting direction to the concept.
5. Ask focused questions when available information is insufficient; distinguish confirmed facts from assumptions.
